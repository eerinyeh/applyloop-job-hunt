# Eligibility and sourcing

Checked 7 October 2026. These rules can change; recheck primary sources when a later application depends on them.

## Eligibility

Assess the vacancy's exact wording: current work rights, start-date rights, future sponsorship, nationality, clearance, residency, degree timing, location and professional qualifications are separate checks. Quote the relevant requirement in the private decision record.

Keep the candidate's status and dates in the private evidence bank. A planned Graduate grant is not current Graduate permission. Verify the expiry date, current work conditions and nationality when needed by a specific application. Do not copy older work-rights claims automatically.

GOV.UK states that a non-doctoral Graduate visa lasts two years for applications on/before 31 December 2026, and 18 months from 1 January 2027; it starts on approval. It cannot be extended, though another eligible route may be available. Do not infer an expiry year before approval. See https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa . For Student work conditions consult https://www.gov.uk/student-visa and the person's grant conditions.

'No sponsorship required now' and 'no sponsorship required now or in the future' are different questions. Do not choose a misleading answer to avoid an employer filter. Use verified facts and the exact wording, distinguish the employer's definition of sponsorship from immigration-route facts, and ask for clarification when a yes/no form cannot express a conditional situation. Don't speculate that future seniority guarantees sponsorship.

UKSV says insufficient UK residency is not necessarily a bar to clearance, although meaningful checks need sufficient information. Do not automatically reject every SC role based on limited residency. A role requiring existing clearance or a stated, applicable non-waivable residency rule may be a hard gate. Otherwise mark verify with the hiring contact's guidance. See https://www.gov.uk/guidance/united-kingdom-security-vetting-applicant .

Government roles do not all have the same nationality rule. A continent or passport region does not determine eligibility. Use the exact nationality, Civil Service eligibility rules and any reserved-post restriction: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/nationality-rules .

## Job sourcing

Manual pasted JDs and employer career links are the default. Preserve full text, source URL, retrieval date and requisition ID. If the advert/portal is inaccessible, use supplied text and flag eligibility not checked in the portal.

LinkedIn's help prohibits tools that scrape or automate its website: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387 . No free dependable supported LinkedIn job-search scraping API is established here. Do not promise scraping, add login-cookie workarounds or imply that a third-party scraper is an official API. User-pasted ads, saved links and manually exported alert emails are workable inputs. Same approach for Indeed unless an authorized integration is provided.

Some employers publish postings via documented company job-board APIs:

- Greenhouse: https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html . Public GET postings are available for a specified board; this is not a search across LinkedIn or all companies.
- Lever: https://github.com/lever/postings-api . Company-scoped published postings endpoint, including EU host variants. Follow the documentation for pagination and host.

These are suggested future adapters, not implemented integrations in this version. Avoid claiming API engineering experience based on a documented option. Fetching postings doesn't authorize applying, sending emails or bypassing access restrictions.
